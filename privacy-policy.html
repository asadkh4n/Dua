Dua - Privacy Policy

Dua collects no personal data.

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and it only uses internet to stream/download Quran recitations upon request.

Everything you create or change in the app (your settings, your own duas, your daily adhkar list and your counts) is stored only on your phone. If you export your duas, the file is saved where you choose. The app does not send it anywhere.

Permissions the app uses:
• Notifications, to show dua and adhkar reminders.
• Vibration, for the tasbeeh counter.

The built-in duas are included in the app. Reminders are created on your phone.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown here and on the store listing.
